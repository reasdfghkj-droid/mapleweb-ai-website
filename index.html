Quick answers

Questions before we build?

How much does a website cost?

Our starting price is $500 CAD for a simple website. We publish six price ranges up to $7,000 CAD. Your final quote depends on the pages, design and features you approve.

When do I pay for my website?

After you receive your completed website. We do not ask for an upfront deposit. You agree on the scope, total price, tax and invoice due date before work starts; payment is requested only after delivery is confirmed. Live Stripe billing is still being set up.

Can I see an example before deciding?

Yes. Use the Build with AI planning chat to describe your business, then preview a sample design. The sample is generated from a fixed template; it is not a finished or published website.

Does the chat automatically send my details?

No. The planning chat helps you create and copy a website plan. Automatic customer submissions are not enabled yet, and nothing is sent without your consent when secure submissions become available.

Will my website go live without my approval?

No. We require customer and owner approval of the current design before publishing any customer website. Pricing, payments and timelines are reviewed before work starts.
